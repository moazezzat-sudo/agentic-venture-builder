# Phase 1, step 9: customer interview kit (The Mom Test style), after go

For: HR teams in Cairo SMEs (10‑200 employees) struggling with manual, error‑prone interview scheduling

Ask about past behaviour; don't describe the idea or ask whether they would buy it. Questions from: model.

1. How do you currently organize interview slots for candidates? Walk me through the last hiring round.
2. What tools or spreadsheets do you use to coordinate interview times, and how much time does that take each week?
3. Can you tell me about the biggest mistake or mix‑up that’s happened because of the current scheduling process?
4. How do interviewers and candidates confirm or change interview times today, and what frustrations do you hear from them?
5. When you need to add or remove an interview slot, what steps are involved and who has to be notified?
6. What cost (in hours or money) do you associate with the current scheduling workflow, and how do you track it?
7. Have you ever looked for or tried a dedicated scheduling tool? What did you like or dislike about it?
